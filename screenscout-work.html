The turning point

"A national number hid where the demand was."

We started by forecasting revenue. In a weekly check-in, the sponsor's programming lead explained why that fails: A film that looks small nationally gets dropped everywhere, including the few theaters where it would sell out. We rebuilt the output around his decision.

One revenue forecast per film

→A High / Medium / Low film rating

→A tier for every theater

→Play / Weekends / Skip, with cutoffs his team controls

What I changed, and why

Five decisions. The first came from the people who would use it.

01

Ratings and tiers, not one national forecast.

One national number per film

→A film rating, plus a tier for every theater

02

Rating × tier lookup table

→Play, Weekends or Skip, decided theater by theater

The table contradicted itself, so we stopped using it.

03

→Flag it: No cast data for this film

What I'd do differently
