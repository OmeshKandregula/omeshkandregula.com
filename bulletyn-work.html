What I changed, and why

Four decisions from the beta. The first one removed the feature I had been proudest of.

01

The personalization note learned to stay quiet.

37% of notes decline to write, on purpose

The feature that made the product worth building was the one quietly driving people away. The fix was to make it speak less, and to track the silence rate so it cannot drift either way.

539 of 1,474 notes · 14 days

02

Zero publisher sentences.

Every description is written from scratch. A legal exposure most newsletters ignore, enforced by the pipeline rather than by good intentions.

→Shares a seven word run with the source, rejected

→Contains a name or number not in the original, rejected

The second rule is what stops it inventing facts while dodging plagiarism

03

One story, not three versions of it.

Group stories by meaning, using embeddings

→Group by the rare entities they share

Embeddings measure wording, and newsrooms covering one event choose different words on purpose. What survives paraphrase is the cast: The same people, places, and numbers.

04

No quality change ships on instinct.

Every change to story scoring replays against real archived news and is compared to current behavior before it goes live.

System Architecture

Three stages, each an internal gate

1. Ingest & Cache

Nightly cron scans 496 active feeds. Quality filters keep only high-signal articles.

2. Enrich & Personalize

Role-specific "For You" insights per subscriber. Pre-rendered, zero AI calls at send time.

3. Deliver at 7 AM

Checks every 30 minutes for local 7 AM. Pre-built briefs ship via email.

The rebuild

Started on Supabase with a Lovable-generated front end. That got it working. It was not going to survive a real product.

Next.js on AWSInfrastructure as code with SST
Aurora Serverless v2With Drizzle, scaling to zero between runs
Step FunctionsNightly pipeline, stage by stage
Amazon SESDelivery, with bounce and complaint handling
AWS BedrockClaude Haiku, analysis and personalization

Every brief is pre-rendered overnight. Zero AI calls at send time, which is why it lands at 7 AM local instead of whenever a model finishes thinking.

What has not worked yet

13 plays. Ever. By 3 people.

I shipped spoken briefs and I am not going to call that traction. It sits behind a click into a dashboard instead of arriving where people already listen, so the next test is a podcast feed, not more audio.

A distribution problem, not an audio problem
